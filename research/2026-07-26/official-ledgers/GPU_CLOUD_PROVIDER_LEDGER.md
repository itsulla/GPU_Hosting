## Comparisons that require first-hand testing

1. **Availability and capacity:** repeated fresh allocations by region and time; marketplace offer churn; quota failures.
2. **Interruption behavior:** spot/preemptible eviction notice, checkpoint/restart behavior, and whether storage survives.
3. **Cold starts:** Modal, RunPod Serverless, Baseten, Fireworks/Together dedicated or serverless deployment startup and model-load latency.
4. **Throughput and tail latency:** same model, quantization, prompt mix, concurrency, max tokens, region and network path; record p50/p95/p99, not a single run.
5. **Storage and egress:** persistent volume durability, snapshot/restore, filesystem throughput, object-store integration and outbound transfer cost.
6. **Billing reality:** minimum billable duration, idle CPU/RAM charges, volume billing, replicas/autoscaling, free-credit restrictions, taxes and postpaid terms.
7. **Operational surface:** image build time, driver/CUDA compatibility, logs/metrics, health checks, rolling deploys and rollback.
8. **Model/API behavior:** tokenizer accounting, cached-token treatment, streaming, rate limits, batch semantics and model version pinning.
