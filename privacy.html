What the site measures—and what it does not

An independent, open-weight-first field guide for choosing GPU infrastructure, sizing memory, comparing provider economics, and deploying inference safely.

Scope

GPUHosting.Guide loads Google Analytics, Google Fonts, and selected client-side libraries. Affiliate click measurement records the provider, page, CTA position, selected model or tool when available, calculator result when available, a semantic destination category, and relationship type. Destination categories use publisher-defined labels such as gpu_provider and vps_control_plane; query parameters and referral codes are intentionally excluded.

Affiliate click events

When a marked sponsored link is clicked, the site emits an affiliate_click event. The destination field contains only a semantic category label selected by the publisher, not the link URL or hostname. Referral codes, query parameters, credentials, and form contents are not copied into that field.

External services

Fonts, analytics, code libraries, and merchant destinations can receive ordinary request information such as IP address, user agent, referrer, and timestamp under their own policies. Following an external link moves you to that provider's privacy and cookie practices.

Accounts and forms

This static publication does not provide reader accounts, accept payments, or collect contact-form submissions. Browser or analytics storage may still be used by loaded third-party services according to their configuration.

Sensitive information

Never submit passwords, API keys, private source code, customer data, wallet keys, or credentials through a public correction route.
