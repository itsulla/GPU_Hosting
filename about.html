Why this publication exists

An independent, open-weight-first field guide for choosing GPU infrastructure, sizing memory, comparing provider economics, and deploying inference safely.

Independent by design

An independent, open-weight-first field guide for choosing GPU infrastructure, sizing memory, comparing provider economics, and deploying inference safely.

The publication covers self-hostable model identity, weight-residency estimates, runtime headroom, GPU and rack-system specifications, provider pricing, and reproducible deployment patterns. Proprietary APIs appear only as clearly separated comparators.

What readers should expect

  • Primary-source links beside volatile or consequential claims.
  • Explicit checked dates rather than undated “current” language.
  • Clear separation between facts, estimates, account status, and editorial judgment.
  • No paid ranking, sponsored placement, or claim that commission makes a product better.
